Federal E-Rate Product Eligibility Pipeline

AI Workflows · Regulatory Data

A networking hardware manufacturer had no record of which products across 36,000+ unevenly structured catalog pages qualified for federal E-Rate funding. I broke the FCC's proposed FY2027 Eligible Services List into 49 verbatim citable rules, audited all 36,079 indexed product URLs, and built a classification pipeline pairing AI reasoning with deterministic code checks and a plain-English review workbook for edge cases.

Outcome: Produced a verified 26,731-row part-number eligibility dataset (identifying 101 eligible families and 266 eligible standalone parts), loaded directly into a sales prospecting tool for pre-bid outreach and reused for a second manufacturer's catalog.

Pipelines · E-Commerce

Geospatial Site Selection & Multi-Factor Scoring

Geospatial · Decision Systems
