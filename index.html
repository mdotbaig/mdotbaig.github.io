Federal E-Rate product eligibility pipeline

AI Workflows · Regulatory Data

A networking hardware manufacturer had no record of which products across 36,000+ unevenly structured catalog pages qualified for federal E-Rate funding. I broke the FCC's proposed FY2027 Eligible Services List into 49 verbatim, citable rules, audited all 36,079 indexed product URLs, and built a classification pipeline pairing AI reasoning with deterministic code checks and a plain-English review workbook for edge cases.

The pipeline produced a verified eligibility dataset of 26,731 part numbers, identifying 101 qualifying product families and 266 standalone parts. It went into a sales prospecting tool for pre-bid outreach and was reused for a second manufacturer's catalog.

Dialect translation model for a low-resource language

Fine-Tuning · Synthetic Data

I fine-tuned an open 12-billion-parameter translation model with LoRA adapters to translate an Arabic dialect across nine regional sub-dialects. Little usable training text existed, so I built a synthetic data generator whose quality gates reject Modern Standard Arabic vocabulary and duplicate examples, and compared several models to check the generated data.

The model reached 93.8% accuracy on dialect benchmarks and kept the northern and southern sub-dialects distinct, without drifting into Modern Standard Arabic or Egyptian Arabic.

Data center site selection & power feasibility

Geospatial · Energy

I built a site suitability model that scores 6,896 census tracts across Texas on nine weighted factors, among them power infrastructure, fiber connectivity, water, natural hazards, natural gas, and workforce. Python and QGIS pipelines handle the spatial indexing, joins, and proximity analysis. For a greenfield build, I identified substations with spare capacity, assessed adjacent land, and compared buying and leasing on an all-in rate per kilowatt-hour.

I worked with 131+ stakeholders, from substation owners and utilities to municipalities and the grid operator, and cleared blockers such as missing meter identifiers before any capital was committed.
